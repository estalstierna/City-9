# City 09 // Design Bible

Alla designregler på ett ställe. Del A är **kanon**: en text på engelska att klistra in före en bildprompt, eller att ge en bild-AI som bakgrund. Del B är **mallar** per bildtyp, där bara [hakparenteserna] ska fyllas i. Del C är **kontrollistan** att gå igenom innan en bild godkänns.

Detaljerade prompter för enskilda bilder finns kvar i `docs/style-sheet.md`, och dess avsnitt 5o (promptmallen) pekar hit. Om något skiljer sig gäller den här filen, eftersom den samlar de senaste besluten.

## Del A: Kanon (klistra in)

```
CITY 09 — DESIGN CANON. Follow every rule below exactly.

WORLD
- City 09: an occupied Eastern-European river city under the Universal Union. A five-arch hydroelectric dam, a dark river, grey Soviet-era apartment blocks ("the Stacks"), and on the far bank a colossal, sheer, blade-like dark metallic SPIRE ribbed with faint cold-blue lines, piercing low cloud.
- Mood: cold, overcast, worn, quiet fear. Brutalist concrete, plastic-taped windows, laundry lines, rooftop antennas, chalk on walls. Never neon cyberpunk, never modern cars, never clean or glossy.
- The resistance mark is a hand-chalked lambda (λ). Civil police NEVER wear a lambda.

GLOBAL STYLE
- Gritty semi-realistic dystopian concept art, painterly realism with crisp linework, subtle film grain.
- Desaturated steel-grey and slate-blue palette, sharp cyan accent #5FC8DD (lenses, status lights, screens).
- Cold overcast light from upper left. No lens flare, no motion blur.
- Worn materials: scuffed ceramic, padded ballistic nylon, oiled leather, chipped enamel, faded canvas.
- Text in images: condensed military stencil capitals, off-white #E8E6DF, spelled exactly as written in quotes. Only the text that is asked for.

CIVIL PROTECTION (CCA) — THE UNIT
- HELMET: off-white to pale bone-grey ceramic full-head gas-mask helmet, a smooth featureless skull dome with a subtle raised seam brow to crown; two large perfectly round eye openings rimmed in thick black rubber with dark smoked lenses that have a faint cyan sheen; a TWO-PART respirator: a round bolted gunmetal respirator plate on the mask at the mouth, and directly beneath it a short round filter canister hanging towards the throat — two stacked discs, as on the approved helmet sheet; ONE rounded earmuff headset cup over the LEFT ear with ONE short stub antenna; dark knit balaclava at the neck seal. Nobody ever sees a unit's face unless the mask is off.
- BODY: dark charcoal-navy padded tactical jacket with a tall zipped stand-up collar; a fitted quilted ballistic vest with three thick horizontal padded ribs and a centre zip; a white vertical rectangular double-bar patch on the left chest; light grey shoulder panels with a thin division-colour edge stripe; two stacked black duty belts with flat brushed-silver buckles, a radio and a pouch with tiny cyan status lights; black gloves with pale grey wrist straps; dark grey-green trousers with a thin side stripe bloused into tall black side-zip boots.
- ARMBAND (left upper arm): a WRAPAROUND cloth band, never a square patch; heavy woven fabric about 10 cm tall, double-stitched edges, black velcro tabs. Layout: small circular embroidered division glyph on the LEFT, "C9:D<district>" in bold stencil in the CENTRE, rank insignia on the RIGHT. Rank bars are always VERTICAL, never horizontal.
- DESIGNATION PATCH: on the left upper arm, directly ABOVE the armband, a small sewn rectangular cloth patch in dark charcoal with a thin overlocked edge, slightly rounded corners, about twice as wide as the armband is tall. Exactly two left-aligned lines of condensed off-white stencil capitals, both the same height, each about half the height of the armband's "C9:D5": line one "CCA.C<city>.D<district>", line two "<DIVISION>-<RANK>-<5 digits>". Nothing else on the patch. Never on the chest, never on the back, never printed on the jacket, never on one line. The district on the armband matches the patch ("C9:D5" with "CCA.C9.D5").
- EMBLEM (every division, same frame): a brushed-steel ring with bevelled edges; the UPPER third of the ring is a separate arc in the division colour; a sharp steel V-chevron points DOWNWARD and breaks through the top of the ring; behind it a faint wireframe globe in dark steel; in the centre a round medallion with the division glyph in bone-white relief; narrow riveted vertical struts either side of the chevron. CCA emblems are ROUND. (Overwatch is hexagonal.)

DIVISIONS — colour (armband, emblem arc, shoulder stripe, officer crest stripe) · glyph · character
- UNION — civil protection blue #2F6FB5 · upright stun baton with two arcs · street patrol, order; rain-darkened, scuffed, upright and watchful.
- HELIX — clinical white #E9EEF0 with teal #3FB8A8 trim (emblem arc teal) · double helix strand · medics; white band on the left forearm, white hard-shell satchel, biogel canisters; clean but with scrubbed-pale old blood.
- JURY — judgement crimson #A3262A · cracked human skull · military police and interrogation; near-black uniform, near-black lenses with a red glint, black leather gauntlets to the elbow, data slate; immaculate, still, severe.
- RAZOR — hardpoint gold #C9A13B · kite shield split by a vertical blade · VIP and hardpoint protection; rigid chest and thigh plates, heavier pauldrons, compact SMG, riot shield; polished, wide-stanced, immovable.
- SPEAR — hazard orange #D9641E · spearhead piercing a broken hazard triangle · assault, 404 quarantine zones; twin side filters instead of one canister, flip-down visor bar, chest rig, shotgun, taped boots; ash, soot, scorch marks, mud to the knees.
- VECTOR — chemical yellow-green #9DB23A · cracked hexagonal cell held shut by a bolted clamp · necrotic containment and research; sealed rubberised suit over the uniform, twin filters, decon lance, specimen case; bleach splashes, chemical stains.
- GRID — burnished copper #B5652E · lightning bolt through a hexagonal nut · infrastructure: forcefields, scanners, conduits, locks; welding visor, copper bands, power cell on the back, multi-driver and diagnostic slate; scorch marks, grease.
- OA (Officer Academy) — command violet #6B4FA0 · three ascending chevrons under a five-point star · officer training; long dark double-breasted greatcoat over the vest, peaked collar with silver pins, single officer belt with cross strap; crisp and tailored.
- VEIL — no colour at all, black on black · an EMPTY medallion (a ring with nothing inside) · officially does not exist; OFFICERS ONLY. Its own helmet, "THE BLANK": a close-fitting matte black shell with NO visible eyes — a flush band of densely perforated black mesh across the eye line that never glows or reflects; no mouth canister, two flat filter cartridges along the jaw; a flush ear disc with a short stub antenna on the left; a gloss-black crest stripe; a faint embossed empty circle on the forehead; a soft black hood. A fitted matte black tactical uniform (softshell jacket, low-profile plate carrier, cargo trousers, knee pads, soft-soled boots) — never a coat. Armband matte charcoal with an empty grey circle, a BLANK centre (no city, no district) and officer rank in dull grey thread; the designation patch is covered by a strip of black tape; the emblem only inside the collar.

RANKS (armband right side; helmet)
- RcT recruit: grey armband, "C9:D5" and "RcT", no bars; no mask — grey training uniform, shaved head.
- Enlisted 05 / 04 / 03 / 02 / 01: division-colour armband with 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 thin VERTICAL silver bars. Standard helmet, NO crest stripe.
- EpU (Elite Protection Unit, the field elite between the grades and the officers): 1 wide vertical silver bar. Its OWN ARMOURED HELMET: a gunmetal plasteel shell clamped over the ceramic mask — armoured brow ridge, cheek guards, a riveted plasteel collar around the two-part respirator, polished dark steel rings round the lenses, a thin silver line over the crown. A plate carrier with plasteel plates and articulated shoulder guards (division-colour edge); a personal suppressed MP7A1 with a holographic sight. Officers give the armoured helmet back for the crest stripe.
- OfC / SqL / DvL (officers): 1 wide vertical silver bar + 1 / 2 / 3 silver five-point stars. Helmet: a crest stripe brow to crown in the division colour, edged both sides with a fine silver line.
- AdC Adjutant Commander / CmD Field Commander / SeC Sectorial Commander (High Command, above divisions): BLACK armband with a gold edge, a gold V-chevron glyph, "C9:D1" in gold, 1 wide gold bar + 1 / 2 / 3 gold stars. Their OWN HELMET (not the bone-white one): a smooth, elongated full-head shell of polished dark graphite with a faceted brow ridge; deep-set round lenses glowing soft warm gold inside thin gold bezels; a narrow VERTICAL mouth grille with five gold slats instead of a canister; a flat round filter disc flush in each cheek; one earmuff cup with a stub antenna on the left; a high rolled black cowl collar. Rank also on the helmet: 1 / 2 / 3 thin gold lines brow to crown. Long black greatcoat with gold piping. Designation patch black with gold text and edge, division field "HC": "CCA.C9.D1" / "HC-CmD-00417". Never a division colour.
- EA (Earth Administrator): no armband, no helmet; shown only as a seal — a gold laurel wreath round the CCA chevron.
- Count bars and stars exactly.

CITIZENS
- BASE: hip-length faded blue-teal denim-like canvas work jacket #3E5F6E, boxy, short mandarin stand collar, a row of seven small dark grey buttons, no breast pockets; dirty off-white crew-neck undershirt; faded dark indigo work trousers; scuffed brown work shoes. Faces undernourished, unsmiling, no makeup, no jewellery.
- CID LABEL: on the wearer's RIGHT chest (left side of the image), a small off-white cloth tape label, "CID" + five digits in black stencil, a tiny barcode beneath. Every citizen has a different number.
- COMMENDED (loyalist): same uniform, newer, fitted, buttoned to the top, pressed; bright white label with a thin gold line; a round enamel commendation pin above the label (gold rim, slate-blue centre, tiny V-chevron); a gold-striped priority ration card.
- UNDER WATCH: older, torn, darned; the CID label overprinted with a red #A3262A diagonal stripe and a small red "OBS"; a large faded red stencilled circle with a slash and "D5" on the back; a grey monitoring band on the left wrist with a blinking cyan light.
- CWU (Civil Workers' Union): an ochre-yellow work jacket #B8902F over the citizen jacket, a grey reflective band, a CWU badge on the chest, and a CID label with a yellow stripe and "CWU". Supervisor: add a small gold chevron on the badge and a peaked cap.
- CANAL CELLS (resistance): citizen clothes worn inside out or patched, scarves and work gloves, mismatched scavenged gear; the lambda is sewn INSIDE the collar, never on an armband.

OVERWATCH (OTA) — military, transhuman, never civil police
- Never the ceramic gas-mask helmet, never cloth armbands. All markings are PAINTED on armour and HEXAGONAL.
- Emblem: a flat-topped graphite hexagon, the Union V-chevron with a circle in its notch, and a narrow horizontal visor slit across it — cyan (infantry), red (elite), gold (command).
- SOLDIER: dark graphite closed helmet with two angular cyan lenses; padded slate-blue and grey armour; one cyan chevron on the shoulder plate; chest stencil "C9-OTA 4471"; pulse rifle with a cyan cell.
- ELITE: white sculpted helmet with one narrow horizontal RED sensor band; white segmented armour, black joints; red chevron and red edge on the shoulder plate; "C9-OTA-E 0019"; red energy cell.
- AIRWATCH: Overwatch's air branch; the hexagon flanked by two swept rotor blades. The SHRIKE gunship: tandem pilot/gunner, coaxial rotors, chin turret, rocket pods, a troop bay amidships with sliding doors on both sides, "AW-S 09-2". Citizens call its rotor sound "the knock".

MACHINES
- PT-V (CCA patrol vehicle): 6x6, wedge nose with a crowd plough, vision slits, roof cupola with scanner mast, blue band, CCA emblem on the doors, "PT-V D5-07".
- OTA transport: same hull, composite armour, one cyan visor slit, remote turret, hexagon emblem, "OTA-T 09-3", no blue.
- SC-9 "LANTERN" scanner drone (GRID): a slim vertical HEXAGONAL body about 60 cm tall in gunmetal ceramic; two ducted fans on short arms with copper cowlings; a rotating collar of SIX slit lenses (cyan patrol / red alert); a camera turret with an iris underneath; a flash bar of FOUR xenon cells; a whip antenna and speaker grille on top. Never a sphere with one big lens.

THE NECROTIC (404 zones)
- An original infection, not crabs or head-parasites. It enters through a bite and grows along the spine: grey, fibrous, like wet ash and lichen. It NEVER glows.
- EARLY: grey threads spreading from a bite; the host still looks like a person. MIMIC: the growth has split the back; the host repeats the last voice it heard. BLOOM: head and shoulders fused into a hood of grey plates; it stands still, listening. No gore close-ups.

THE MAN FROM CITY 17
- Never named, never shown with a face. On broadcast screens only as a heavily blurred figure in a dark doorway with the caption "ANTICITIZEN ONE".

NEVER
- The words "Half-Life", "Combine", "Metrocop" or any existing game's names, logos, characters, exact designs or insignia.
- Logos of real brands. Neon cyberpunk. Cartoon or anime style. Gore.
- A single respirator canister on a civil police helmet: it is always two stacked discs (plate + filter beneath). Exceptions: SPEAR and VECTOR wear twin filters at the sides of the jaw; High Command has the gold grille; VEIL has jaw cartridges.
- A lambda on anything a unit wears. A face on a masked unit. A designation on the chest, the back or on one line. Horizontal rank bars.
```

## Del C: Kontrollista innan en bild godkänns

1. **Patch:** sitter den ovanför armbandet, i två rader, med rätt stad, distrikt, division, rang och fem siffror?
2. **Armband:** är det ett band runt armen? Stämmer glyfen till vänster, "C9:D_" i mitten och rätt antal stående streck och stjärnor till höger? Stämmer distriktet med patchen?
3. **Hjälm:** har manskap ingen rand? Har officerare en rand i divisionsfärg med silverkant? Har ledningen grafit och guld? Finns exakt en hörlurskåpa med kort antenn, på vänster öra?
4. **Division:** stämmer färgen med hexkoden? Finns divisionens kännetecken med (HELIX underarmsband, JURY handskar, RAZOR plåtar, SPEAR dubbla filter, VECTOR dräkt, GRID koppar och kraftcell, OA kappa)?
5. **Ingen lambda** på något en enhet bär, och **inget ansikte** under en mask.
6. **Medborgare:** sitter CID-etiketten på bärarens högra bröst (vänster i bild), och är numret unikt?
7. **Overwatch:** är formerna sexkantiga och målade, utan tygarmband och utan CCA-hjälm?
8. **Text:** finns bara den text som efterfrågades, och är den rätt stavad?
9. **Ingen kopia** av befintliga spel, och inga varumärken.
10. **Porträtt:** sitter hjälmen eller ansiktet centrerat med luft ovanför, som Vigil och Censor?
